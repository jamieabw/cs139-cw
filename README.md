## Overview
This is a website allowing you to create and join groups, where you can then share and split bills easily between eachother. You can pay a bill, submit
this to the group and share it however you want between the members, which will then notify them through the website and through email. The receivers of the
splits can then settle their debts by submitting their evidence of payment, which you can then acknowledge or reject. 

## Features
- creating / joining password-secured groups
- creating bills
- proportional splitting of bills between members of the group by the creator of the bill
- the bills can be edited after being sent
- account registration / authentication
- account settings such as changing username, email or password
- viewing an account's current/previous/archived debts
- viewing the status of bills and payments
- notifications through both the website and your associated email address
- account recovery through code sent to your email
- easy to navigate and self-explanatory pages
- settling debts with image evidence, where the creator can acknowledge or accept the payment
- archiving and deletion of bills
- admin features like user logs, bill logs and full access to all groups/bills

## Flair
For one substantial flair feature, I decided to implement proportional bill splitting on both creation of the bill and editing of the bill. This allows
different members to not be included in specific bills and also make specific users pay more or less depending on the bill creator's desires.

## How to access admin account
The admin account is any account which has the username 'admin' (usernames are unique). Once an account has been created with the username, multiple new
tabs on the nav bar will be accessible, all groups will be accessible aswell as all bills. The bill logs will be contained on the pages of the specific bills.
